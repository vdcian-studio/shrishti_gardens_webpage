The High Sanctuary

13 EXCLUSIVE DWELLINGS

Nestled amidst the breathtaking landscapes of Kodaikanal, Shrishti Gardens offers a peaceful escape surrounded by nature. Experience the beauty of the hills, the serenity of the surroundings, and the comfort of a memorable stay.

Cradled at over 2,100 metres altitude in the cool misty Western Ghats, our retreat is designed to celebrate the stillness of old-world Kodai. Here, mornings awaken to whispering pine needles and the scent of wild eucalyptus, while twilight settles over mountain contours with warmth and hospitality.
